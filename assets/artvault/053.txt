title: Name mangling
---
   source:    namespace foo { void bar(); }

   symbol:    _ZN3foo3barEv
               | || | || | |
               | || | || | +-- v : takes no arguments (void)
               | || | || +---- E : end of the nested name
               | || | |+------ bar (3 characters)
               | || | +------- 3 : length of the next name
               | || +--------- foo
               | |+----------- 3
               | +------------ N : a nested name follows
               +-------------- _Z : a mangled C++ name

   c++filt turns it back into foo::bar()
