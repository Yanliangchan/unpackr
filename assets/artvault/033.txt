title: UPX in a PE
---
   unpacked                        packed with UPX

   +----------+                    +----------+
   | headers  |                    | headers  |
   +----------+                    +----------+
   | .text    |                    | UPX0     |  raw size 0, virtual size big
   | .rdata   |                    +----------+  (room to decompress into)
   | .data    |                    | UPX1     |  stub + compressed sections
   +----------+                    +----------+
   | .rsrc    |                    | UPX2     |  resources
   +----------+                    +----------+

   the section names are only labels; the empty-on-disk, large-in-memory
   executable section is the shape that matters
