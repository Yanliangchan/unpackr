title: What a packer stub does
---
   entry point
       |
       v
   +---------------------------------------------+
   | 1  resolve a few APIs (or none)             |
   | 2  allocate or reuse memory                 |
   | 3  decompress / decrypt the real program    |
   | 4  fix imports and relocations              |
   | 5  jump to the original entry point (OEP)   |
   +---------------------------------------------+
       |
       v
   the program as the compiler made it

   until step 5 the file's imports and strings describe the stub, not the app
