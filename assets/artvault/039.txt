title: Little endian
---
   the 32-bit value 0x12345678 at address 0x1000:

        address   0x1000  0x1001  0x1002  0x1003
        byte        78      56      34      12
                    ^ least significant byte first

   so the bytes  DE AD BE EF  read as the number 0xEFBEADDE

   network byte order is the opposite (big endian), hence htons and htonl
