title: Two's complement
---
   8-bit values              negate = invert every bit, then add 1

    5  = 0000 0101                5 = 0000 0101
   -5  = 1111 1011           invert = 1111 1010
                                 +1 = 1111 1011   = -5

   the top bit is the sign; the range is -128 .. 127

   0xFF as a signed byte is -1 and as an unsigned byte is 255, which is how
   one comparison can be right and the next one wrong
