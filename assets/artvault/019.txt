title: Two views of an ELF
---
        linking view                         execution view
        (section headers)                    (program headers)

     +------------------+                 +----------------------+
     | .text            |--+              | PT_LOAD   r-x        |
     | .rodata          |--+------------->|  .text .rodata       |
     +------------------+                 +----------------------+
     | .data            |--+              | PT_LOAD   rw-        |
     | .bss             |--+------------->|  .data .bss          |
     +------------------+                 +----------------------+
     | .symtab .strtab  |  (not loaded)   | PT_INTERP, PT_DYNAMIC|
     +------------------+                 +----------------------+

   the loader reads only the right-hand side: a file with its section headers
   stripped or damaged still runs, which is why packers remove them
