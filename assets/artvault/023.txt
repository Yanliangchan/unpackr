   +----------+----------+----------+----------+----------------+
                                      last section ends here ^

   the loader ignores everything after the last section, but the program can
   read its own file

   legitimate: Authenticode signatures, installers, self-extracting archives
   also seen:  droppers carrying an encrypted payload
