title: XOR without the key
---
   encrypt each byte with one key byte:   e = p ^ k

   the key cancels between neighbours:

      e1 ^ e2 = (p1 ^ k) ^ (p2 ^ k) = p1 ^ p2

   so for "http" under any key:

      'h' ^ 't' = 0x68 ^ 0x74 = 0x1C        key 0x5A:  0x32 ^ 0x2E = 0x1C
                                            key 0x41:  0x29 ^ 0x35 = 0x1C

   searching for the pattern of differences finds the text without
   trying a single key
